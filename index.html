Meet the founders

Liu & Tree Capital is an investment management firm founded on a shared conviction: that enduring investment excellence emerges from intellectual independence, scientific rigor, and disciplined execution.

Founded by Liu and Tree, the firm brings together quantitative research, advanced technology, and a systematic approach to capital allocation. We seek to uncover meaningful patterns within complex markets, transform insight into measurable decisions, and pursue resilient performance across changing market environments.

We believe that in a world defined by uncertainty, lasting advantage belongs not to those who predict every outcome, but to those who build the discipline to navigate them.

Liu & Tree Capital 由 Liu 与 Tree 两位创始人共同创立，秉持独立思考、科学严谨与纪律执行的核心理念，致力于构建具有长期生命力的系统化投资体系。

我们以量化研究为基础，以先进技术为驱动，将数学模型、数据科学与严谨的资本配置方法相结合，探索复杂市场中的深层结构，并将研究洞见转化为可验证、可执行的投资决策。

我们相信，面对充满不确定性的金融市场，真正持久的竞争优势，并非源于对每一次市场变化的准确预测，而是源于理解风险、尊重规律，以及在变化中保持理性的能力。

Co-founders
